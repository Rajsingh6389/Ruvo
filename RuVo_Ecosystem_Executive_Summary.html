1. Ecosystem Architecture & Platforms

RuVo is an integrated hyperlocal commerce, delivery, and business management ecosystem engineered to empower local economies.

📱 RuVo Mobile Discover

Customer marketplace for local discovery, multi-shop orders, cart checkout, and real-time delivery GPS tracking.

🛵 RuVo Partner Move

Fulfillment app with live GPS broadcasting, instant assignment acceptance, active run routing, and wallet earnings.

🏪 RuVo Shop Grow

Merchant dashboard for inventory, order fulfillment stages, delivery partner assignment, and platform fee settlements.

💻 RuVo Admin Web Connect

Centralized operations portal for approvals, partner KYC, commission audits, refund processing, and support helpdesk.

All RuVo products share a single, recognizable motion signature: The RuVo Flow. Total duration is calibrated at 2.45 seconds.
